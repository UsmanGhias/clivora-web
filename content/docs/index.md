- **Freelancer portal** — earnings, projects, proposals, invoices, time tracking, CRM
- **Client portal** — post jobs, review proposals, manage spend, hire via Connect
- **CLIVORA Connect** — private marketplace with $0 platform fees on negotiated rates
- **Admin console** — platform operations for CODCrafters
