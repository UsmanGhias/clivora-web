# User guide

This guide explains how to use CLIVORA in plain language, for freelancers and clients.

---

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [clivora.io/signup](https://clivora.io/signup) or install the **Android app**.
2. Choose **Freelancer** or **Client** at signup. This choice is locked to your email (you cannot switch later on the same account).
3. Verify your email when prompted.
4. Sign in on web or app with the **same account**.

**Tip:** Use Google sign-in on web if you prefer one-click login.

---

## 2. Freelancer workflow

### Add clients
- Web: **App → Clients**  
- App: **Clients** tab  
- Free plan: up to **3 clients**. Pro: unlimited.

### Create projects
- Link a project to a client, set budget and status.  
- Free plan: up to **5 projects**. Pro: unlimited.

### Send invoices
- Create an invoice, add line items, send a share link to your client.  
- Free plan: **10 invoices per month**. Pro: unlimited.

### Use the dashboard
- See paid revenue, outstanding amounts, and quick actions.

### Hub (app-first features)
On web, **App → Hub** shows tools that work best in the Android app: AI assistant, file vault, automations, rich PDF, time tracking, and more. Use **Get app** to install or open the app.

---

## 3. Client workflow

Clients see a **slate-themed** portal focused on work shared with them.

### What clients can do
- View shared **projects** and **invoices**
- **Messages** with their freelancer
- **Activity** feed of updates
- **Account** settings and plan

### Connect (Pro Plus)
- Post **job needs** so freelancers can apply
- Browse talent and send Connect requests

---

## 4. CLIVORA Connect (marketplace)

**Browse for free.** Contacting and posting require **Pro Plus** ($19/mo).

### For freelancers
1. Upgrade to Pro Plus.
2. Go to **Connect → Update my profile** (or `/connect/manage`).
3. Fill in title, headline, bio, skills, rate, location, availability.
4. Turn **Listed** on to appear on the public board.
5. Browse **Jobs** and apply, or wait for clients to contact you.

### For clients
1. Upgrade to Pro Plus.
2. Go to **Connect → Post a job**.
3. Add title, summary, skills, budget band.
4. Browse **Talent** and send Connect requests.

### How contact works (private by design)
1. You send a **Connect request** with a short message.
2. The other person sees it in **Inbox**.
3. If they **Accept**, email addresses are revealed.
4. Continue the relationship in CLIVORA CRM (clients, projects, invoices).

**Why this matters:** Unlike open marketplaces, your email is not public on day one.

---

## 5. Messages

- Freelancers: **App → Messages** (web) or Messages in app.
- Clients: client messages screen in portal.
- Chat syncs via Supabase when online.

---

## 6. Upgrade plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Trying CLIVORA, small workload |
| **Pro** | $9/mo | Unlimited CRM, advanced app tools |
| **Pro Plus** | $19/mo | Everything in Pro + **Connect** posting & contact |

- **Android:** Subscribe in app via Google Play.  
- **Web:** [clivora.io/pro](https://clivora.io/pro), submit payment proof (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank). Admin approves and unlocks your plan.

**Already on Pro?** The web checkout will not ask you to pay for Pro again, only Pro Plus upgrade if you want Connect.

**Already on Pro Plus?** You see an “already unlocked” message, no extra payment.

---

## 7. Account restrictions

Admins can set accounts to:

| Status | What happens |
|--------|----------------|
| **Blocked** | Cannot use the app; redirected to blocked page |
| **Restricted** | Can browse Connect; cannot post or send contact requests |

Contact support if you believe this is a mistake: [contact@clivora.io](mailto:contact@clivora.io).

---

## 8. Android app tips

- **Offline:** CRM data works offline; syncs when you sign in online.
- **Deep links:** Links from web can open the app (`clivora://` scheme).
- **Notifications:** Enable push for invoice and message alerts.
- **Backup:** Export workspace from Settings (app).

---

## 9. Web app tips

- Use **Back to app** and breadcrumbs on Connect and Pro pages.
- **Smart app CTA** in the header: installs Play Store app if missing, or opens app if installed.
- Cloud CRM on web mirrors Supabase, best for quick edits; heavy work stays in Android.
