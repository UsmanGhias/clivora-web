# Product overview

CLIVORA is a **business operating system** for freelancers and the clients who hire them. It combines:

1. **CRM**, clients, projects, invoices, expenses, tasks  
2. **CLIVORA Connect**, a private marketplace to hire talent or find work  
3. **Client portal**, clients see shared projects, invoices, and messages  
4. **Web + Android**, one Supabase account across both

---

## Who CLIVORA is for

### Freelancers
Designers, developers, writers, consultants, and small agencies who need one place to manage clients, send invoices, track projects, and find new work on Connect.

### Clients
People or businesses hiring freelancers. They get a client portal (slate theme on web) to view shared work, pay invoices, and post jobs on Connect.

---

## What makes CLIVORA different

- **Private contact**, emails stay hidden until both sides accept a Connect request
- **Marketplace + CRM together**, after you match, keep working in the same clients, projects, and invoices workspace
- **One account**, web and Android stay in sync
- **Offline-ready Android**, keep working without a connection, then sync

---

## Main product areas

### Dashboard
See revenue, outstanding invoices, quick actions, and active projects at a glance.

### Clients
Store contact details, tags, notes, and link them to projects and invoices.

### Projects
Track budget, status, deadlines, milestones, and time (Android app).

### Invoices
Create branded invoices, share links with clients, track payments, recurring invoices (app).

### Connect marketplace
Browse talent and jobs, send Connect requests, manage your profile or job posts (Pro Plus).

### Messages
Chat between freelancer and linked clients (cloud-synced).

### Reports & analytics
Basic on free; exports and advanced analytics on Pro (mainly in Android app).
